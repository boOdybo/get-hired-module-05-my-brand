# Module 05 — Slide Storyboard (Case-Based Revision)

**Total slides:** 58

## Instructional design
A single fictional learner, Maryam Ahmed, is introduced immediately after the cover. The same case facts are carried through the full module. The seven CV sections are explained and applied individually; after each application, the finished section is displayed in CV form. The complete text CV and Canva-formatted preview assemble the same facts.

**Repeated learning loop:** Explain → identify evidence → demonstrate prompt or writing decision → show the exact section output → check factual support.

## Fixed design and delivery rules
- Arabic-first explanation; English technical terms, tool names and CV content remain in English with Arabic context.
- One continuous fictional case; do not change the candidate facts between examples.
- Every one of the seven CV sections has its own output slide immediately after the application.
- Missing information remains a placeholder or is omitted; no invented institution, date, employer, contact detail, metric or certification.
- Larger projector typography, responsive layouts and vertical scrolling where needed on mobile.
- Presentation / Study / Trainer modes, searchable overview, keyboard navigation and fullscreen.
- No per-slide time allocation or session-map filler.
